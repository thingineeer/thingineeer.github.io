English

Lucky Squadron

A vertical shooting roguelike you play with one finger. Drag to fly an animal pilot's fighter through stages of 15 waves. Pick one of three skills on each level-up, power up with weapon breakthroughs, and open supply crates for upgrades that last the run. Grow your squadron with pilots, fighters, equipment and research. No account needed and playable offline. Ads appear only while you are online. Available for iOS, iPadOS and Android in Korean and English.
