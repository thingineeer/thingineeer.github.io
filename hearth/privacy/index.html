Hearth Privacy Policy

Scope and local data

Hearth is an iPad virtual fireplace app. It has no account system and no ads, it does not read the advertising identifier, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It collects anonymous usage statistics to improve the app, and you can turn that off at any time in Settings. Settings, favorites, saved ambiance profiles, and the in-app usage history stay on the device. Usage history can be cleared with Reset statistics in Stats, saved profiles can be deleted from their list, and deleting the app removes everything.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see which features are actually used, Hearth collects anonymous usage events and diagnostics through Firebase Analytics, provided by Google LLC. These are in-app actions such as screen views, when a fire is started and stopped, session length, theme and sound selections, timer use, and purchase starts and completions. Each event carries the app version, iPadOS version, device model, screen orientation, language, approximate country or region, a random identifier generated for each app installation.

Not collected: your name, email address, account information, location coordinates, photos, error report text, or the advertising identifier (IDFA). IP addresses are used only to derive an approximate region and are not stored. The data is used for product improvement only, never for advertising, profiling, or tracking across other apps, and it is not combined with data from other companies. Hearth does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Google's privacy policy applies to Firebase Analytics, and collected events are deleted after 14 months.

To turn it off, switch off Share anonymous usage data in Settings. Collection stops immediately, the setting is kept across launches, and no feature becomes unavailable. It is on by default.

Location, weather, purchases, and on-device intelligence

If you use temperature, Hearth requests approximate location permission and asks Apple WeatherKit for current weather. Hearth does not send coordinates to or store them on an operator-controlled server. Through an App Group, the widget receives the latest temperature, location label, weather symbol, update time, temperature unit, and widget theme. Purchases and restores are processed by Apple StoreKit. Mood suggestions are processed on device with Apple Foundation Models on supported devices.

Support and error reports

Hearth never sends an error report automatically. If you choose to continue through Mail or Share and complete the send action, the recipient may receive your description, occurrence time, selected photos, app and iPadOS versions, device model, selected theme and sound, and sender email address. Reports do not include location coordinates, raw logs, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers.

Messages sent to the support address are kept only as needed to respond and investigate, then deleted within a reasonable period after the purpose is complete or after a valid deletion request.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke location access and continue using the fireplace; only temperature is affected. Anonymous usage statistics can be switched off in Settings at any time. For access, correction, or deletion requests concerning support information, email dlaudwls1203@gmail.com. Apple services, Google Firebase Analytics, and any Mail or sharing provider you choose operate under their own privacy policies. Hearth does not sell personal information or share it for advertising or tracking.

Children and changes

Hearth is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Additional rights may apply under the laws of your region. When this policy changes materially, the date above is updated and, where needed, the app will let you know.
