3. Advertising and privacy choices

Rewarded ads are displayed when the player chooses them. Result-screen interstitials are limited using the game's run count and whether a rewarded ad was shown in that run. Ads are not displayed when VIP entitlement is verified. Removing ads does not delete existing records or stop separate purchase-verification and restoration requests.

Ad requests depend on native consent status. Google UMP provides required consent forms according to region and configuration; iOS requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission where needed. Advertising Privacy Settings is available in the app's Settings when UMP requires a privacy-options entry point for the region. iOS tracking permission and Android advertising IDs can also be managed in device settings. Your choices can affect advertising methods and available ads without stopping separate services such as purchase verification.

Unity Ads is included in the AdMob mediation configuration. Its SDK disclosures include purchase history and app-usage information for advertising measurement; submitting an ad-creative report may also involve processing the report. Unity's user privacy policy also applies when Unity ads are served. See Google Android SDK disclosures, Google iOS SDK disclosures, Unity's user privacy policy, and Apple's tracking-permission guidance for provider information and choices.

7. Information protection

Purchase verification uses HTTPS and authenticated requests. The server database separates users' read access and blocks direct writes from the app. Operational access is limited to necessary permissions, and retention records are handled separately from ordinary service records. Use the device's protected app storage and security controls, including a screen lock.

8. Children's information

When the app first starts, it asks for the year of birth. The year is stored only on the device and is not sent to the verification server or advertising partners. Anyone who turns 14 or younger in the current year is treated as possibly under 14: for them the app does not use personalized ads or the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, and ad requests carry child-protection settings (child age treatment and under-age-of-consent tags). For every user, the app requests only ads rated for general audiences.

Where consent is required to process information about a child under 14, Article 22-2 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act requires consent from, and verification of, the legal representative. A store's content age rating does not replace this consent. Legal representatives may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of a child's information. If processing without required consent is identified, we stop the relevant processing and take deletion or other action required by law. Statutory protection for children's information
