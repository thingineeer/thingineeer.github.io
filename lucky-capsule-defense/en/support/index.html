2. Errors and progress

Send the details you know: device model, Android or iOS version, app version, approximate time and preceding actions, and the expected and actual behavior. Hide personal information in screenshots.

An ongoing run is saved on your device; its resume save is removed when the run is finally concluded. Settings, collection, and cumulative records remain in the device's game profile. This game data is not synchronized to the app's server. Clearing app data or reinstalling may remove progress and anonymous authentication information, so do not use this as your first troubleshooting step.

3. VIP purchase and restoration

subway_vip_pass is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that removes all ads and unlocks up to 2.5× game speed. “Pass” does not mean a recurring subscription. Check the relevant store's purchase screen for the price and availability. mythic_starter_pack is not offered for sale.

If a charge appears but the benefit is missing, do not purchase repeatedly. Check the store account used for the purchase and use Restore Purchases in the app's Settings. If the problem continues, email the store, product, and approximate purchase date. A complete receipt or card details are not needed in your first message.

Deleting app data, reinstalling, or changing anonymous accounts may prevent association with an earlier purchase. Contact support if restoration fails instead of buying the same product again. We also explain purchase-association consequences when handling deletion requests.

Refund eligibility and procedures depend on the store, item, region, and applicable law. Uninstalling the app is not a refund request.

  • Google Play purchases: Google Play's official refund policy

Email us if the issue needs the developer's review. Approval and completion dates depend on the outcome of the relevant request.

5. Privacy and account deletion

Use “Privacy request — Lucky Capsule Defense” in the email subject and state whether you request access, correction, deletion, or restriction. Include your anonymous UID if already known; otherwise, say it is unknown. We verify the requester or authorized representative using the minimum necessary information.

Within 10 days of receiving the request, we explain access arrangements, action taken, or lawful restriction or refusal with an objection method. If access is lawfully postponed, we also explain the reason. Required contract, payment, and supply records are retained separately for 5 years, and consumer complaint and dispute records for 3 years, limited to necessary information. We distinguish these from records to be deleted. Other records, including ordinary inquiries, are deleted without delay when their purpose is fulfilled, a valid deletion request is received, or the service ends. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Uninstalling is different from deleting server accounts or transaction records. Do not send passwords, ID or refresh tokens, complete receipts or app-storage files, private keys, or full card details. We explain a secure method if additional verification is required.

6. Advertising and consent issues

For an ad issue, describe the device, approximate time, and visible error. Interstitial frequency uses the cumulative run count in the device profile, with exclusions for VIP and rewarded-ad viewing during the same run.

Advertising Privacy Settings is available when UMP requires it for your region. iOS tracking permission and Android advertising IDs can also be managed in device settings. Declining tracking does not automatically cancel purchase-verification or support requests. See Apple's tracking guidance and Unity's user privacy information.
